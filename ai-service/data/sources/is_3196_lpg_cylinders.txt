Document: LPG Cylinders — Specification (demo reference: IS 3196 : 1991)
Page: 21
Clause: 8.3 — Periodic Testing

Summary of scope:
This demo document illustrates the kind of material, construction and
testing requirements a domestic LPG cylinder standard typically covers.

Illustrative parameters covered:
- Material and thickness: minimum steel sheet thickness and permitted
  material grades, chosen to withstand internal pressure over the
  cylinder's service life.
- Valve and fitting requirements: standardised valve threads and fittings
  so cylinders interface safely and consistently with regulators.
- Hydrostatic pressure testing: every cylinder is pressure-tested during
  manufacture to a multiple of its working pressure before it is approved
  for filling.
- Periodic re-testing: cylinders already in circulation must be withdrawn
  and re-tested at a fixed interval (illustrated here as roughly every
  5 years) before they can continue to be refilled.

Illustrative clause 8.3 (Periodic Testing):
A cylinder that has not undergone its due periodic re-test, or that fails
the re-test, may not be refilled or returned to circulation until it is
either successfully re-tested and re-stamped, or condemned and withdrawn.

Who this is relevant to:
Cylinder manufacturers, gas distribution agencies, households using LPG
for cooking, and safety inspectors auditing cylinder fleets.
