Document: Protective Helmets for Two-Wheeler Riders (demo reference: IS 4151 : 2015)
Page: 9
Clause: 6.2 — Impact Absorption

Summary of scope:
This demo document illustrates the kind of protective performance and
construction requirements a two-wheeler helmet standard typically sets.

Illustrative parameters covered:
- Shell strength: the outer shell must resist penetration and distribute
  impact force across its surface rather than concentrating it at a point.
- Impact absorption: the inner liner must limit the peak force transmitted
  to a test headform when the helmet is dropped from a specified height
  onto a rigid anvil.
- Strap and retention system: the chin strap and buckle must hold under a
  defined load without releasing, so the helmet stays on during a fall.
- Field of vision: the helmet's opening must allow a minimum horizontal
  and vertical field of view for safe riding.

Illustrative clause 6.2 (Impact Absorption):
A helmet passes this clause only if the peak acceleration recorded on the
test headform, across all specified impact points, stays below the defined
threshold; exceeding it at even one point fails the whole helmet design.

Who this is relevant to:
Helmet manufacturers seeking certification, two-wheeler riders choosing
protective gear, and transport/road-safety authorities.
